VIDEO UPLOAD SETUP

Upload your final video in this folder with this exact filename:

    birthday-surprise.mp4

Recommended format:
- MP4 video
- H.264 video codec
- AAC audio codec
- 1080p or smaller for faster mobile loading
- Landscape or portrait both work

Once the file is added and the website is published, it will automatically
appear inside the "Open your birthday surprise" section. Until then, the site
shows a gentle placeholder instead of a broken video player.
